Hum aapka koi bhi data kisi server par upload, store ya kisi third party ke saath share nahi karte.

Purane resizers aksar photo ko zabardasti chhota karke dhundhla kar dete hain ya target KB se bahut kam size bana dete hain. Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

Kya photo resize karne par uski quality ya text kharab hoga?

Nahi. SnapMint sabse pehle high JPEG/WEBP quality (95% tak) par target KB match karne ki koshish karta hai aur zaroorat padne par hi gently dimensions adjust karta hai, jisse text aur chehra saaf dikhta hai.

Kya mera data ya photo kisi third party ko jaata hai?

Bilkul nahi. Aapki image aapke phone ya computer se bahar nahi jaati—na kisi server par upload hoti hai aur na hi kisi third party ko share ki jaati hai.

Exact 250KB ya 1MB kaise banayein?

Target Size box mein 250 likh kar KB chunein (ya 1 likh kar MB chunein) aur Compress & Resize Now dabayein. Tool exact target ke ±1–2 KB ke andar file taiyar kar dega.

Kya yeh tool sabhi phones aur computers par free hai?

Haan, SnapMint 100% free hai aur Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac ke sabhi browsers par bina kisi app ke kaam karta hai.
